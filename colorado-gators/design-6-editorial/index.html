Chapter One

A Fish Farm That Became Something Extraordinary

Colorado Gators began in 1977 as a fish farm. In 1987, the first alligators were brought in as "garbage disposals" — a practical solution for a working operation.

What happened next is something no one could have predicted. Those practical alligators became the foundation of a rescue operation that now houses hundreds of reptiles — alligators, crocodiles, caimans, tortoises, turtles, snakes, and iguanas — each one found, abandoned, or seized.

The secret? A geothermal well 2,050 feet deep, flowing at a constant 87 degrees year-round. What makes these creatures thrive at nearly 10,000 feet elevation is pure geological fortune.

The Residents

Hundreds of Rescued Reptiles

Every animal at Colorado Gators has a story. Each one was found in need and given a home where they can thrive.

Albino Alligators
Fewer than 1 in 100 alligators are born with albinism. Colorado Gators has one of the largest collections in the world — a genetic rarity made possible by geothermal warmth.
Morris — Movie Star
Star of "Happy Gilmore" and "The Waterboy." Morris became a cultural icon before his passing — now preserved as taxidermy at the farm where he lived.
Crocodiles & Caiman
Multiple species roam the warm ponds alongside alligators — crocodiles from Africa, caimans from South America, all living side by side in the San Luis Valley.
Tropical Birds
Meet Tuka the cockatoo and our vibrant macaws. African Grey parrots complete the flock — a colorful addition to the farm.

What to Do

Hands-On Experiences

Don't just observe — participate. Colorado Gators offers unique experiences you won't find anywhere else.

Pet an Alligator
Hold and pet a juvenile alligator with direct staff supervision. Suitable for all ages with proper guidance.
Feed the Gators
Buy gator chow and personally feed the alligators. Watch these ancient predators surge from the water.
Pet-Friendly
Well-behaved dogs are welcome. Piper the dog visited and gave her seal of approval (except for the African tortoises).
