The Altitude

0 FT

Alligators are a swamp animal. So it feels wrong to find them here — but that's the whole point. Colorado Gators is a rescue, not a theme park. These animals were surrendered, relocated, or pulled from situations where they could no longer be cared for, and they found a permanent home in the high desert. As far as we know, no one else on Earth keeps alligators at this altitude.

Movie Star

The Legend

Morris, the
movie star

Hollywood · Then the high desert

Before he ever saw the San Luis Valley, Morris spent decades on film and television — an alligator who actually made it to the big screen, with a widely loved cameo in Happy Gilmore.

After a long career, he retired here to Colorado, where he spent his golden years at 7,700 feet, far from the studio lots. He's still the name the whole ranch is built around.

The Mission

Rescue first.
Attraction second.

Colorado Gators began as a fish farm that needed a practical way to handle its fish waste. A few alligators seemed like the obvious answer. What started as logistics turned into a mission: give rescued and surrendered reptiles a place to live.

Today that place is a family-run education center and one of the most unusual stops in the San Luis Valley. Admission keeps the tanks fed and the lights on.

Keep the rescue running

The park is supported by visitors and a community that believes these animals belong somewhere safe. If you can, help it keep going.
