Established 1977

From Fish Farm to Reptile Paradise

What started as a fish farm in 1977 became something extraordinary. In 1987, the first alligators arrived as "garbage disposals." Today, Colorado Gators is home to hundreds of rescued reptiles thriving in the warmth of our 2,050-foot deep geothermal well.

We're not a zoo. We're a rescue. Every animal here has been found, abandoned, or seized. We give them a warm, safe place to live for the rest of their lives.
