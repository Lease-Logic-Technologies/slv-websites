The Clark Legacy

Clark Auto Service isn't just a shop—it's a family legacy. Located on Tremont Street in Alamosa, we bridge the gap between the old guard and the new.

With mechanics who worked on cars since the pre-1950s alongside modern experts in 2015+ platforms, you get the best of both worlds under one roof. Whether you're restoring a T-Bucket or troubleshooting a complex modern sensor array, Chris and Ron have the experience to get it right.

We pride ourselves on transparent pricing, fast service, and the kind of personal touch you don't find at big chain shops.
